Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

NaturaList is published by Sensory.AV. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

Summary

NaturaList is local-first. Personal shopping lists, pantry items, meal plans, budgets, preferences, and shopping history are stored on your device. Optional AI, community, retailer, and collaborative-list features use network services only when you choose to use them. NaturaList does not sell personal information, show behavioral advertising, or track users across other companies' apps and websites.

Information Stored on Your Device

  • Shopping lists, pantry items, meal plans, budgets, templates, favorites, and history
  • Dietary preferences, allergies, nutritional goals, and preferred stores
  • App settings and recent AI conversation history
  • An optional Anthropic API key, stored in the iOS Keychain

Deleting the app removes this local data unless it is retained through an Apple backup. Content intentionally published to the community or shared through a collaborative list is hosted separately and is not removed merely by uninstalling the app.

Optional Account And Hosted Features

Sign in with Apple is required for community and collaborative features. When used, NaturaList processes an Apple account identifier and any profile name you choose to provide. The hosted service stores information needed to provide these features, including:

  • Community profile, shared recipes, recipe images, comments, likes, follows, saves, blocks, and reports
  • Collaborative shopping lists, membership, roles, invitations, and activity history
  • Service records needed for authentication, abuse prevention, reliability, and account deletion

Community recipes and profile information are public to other NaturaList users. Collaborative lists are available only to authorized members. Reports and blocks are not public.

You can delete your hosted NaturaList account from the app. Account deletion removes your profile and associated hosted content, subject to limited retention required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or unresolved moderation reports.

AI Features

AI requests are sent either through NaturaList's hosted service or directly to Anthropic when you provide your own API key. A request can include your prompt and the shopping, pantry, dietary, allergy, or meal context needed to answer it. Anthropic processes these requests under its privacy policy. NaturaList does not use AI conversations for advertising.

Location, Camera, Microphone, And Speech

  • Location is used to find nearby stores. Apple location and mapping services process this information; NaturaList does not retain precise location on its hosted service.
  • Camera access is used only when you scan a barcode or receipt.
  • Microphone and speech recognition are used only when you choose voice input. Audio may be processed by Apple under Apple's applicable privacy terms.

Service Providers

NaturaList uses Apple services for Sign in with Apple, maps, location, speech, purchases, and platform delivery; Cloudflare for hosted API and database infrastructure; and Anthropic for AI processing. Retailer integrations may process search terms, store selection, and shopping-list items when you explicitly use retailer lookup or handoff features.

Sharing And Selling

Sensory.AV does not sell or rent personal information and does not share it with advertisers. Information is shared with service providers only to operate features you request, protect the service, or comply with law. Content you publish to the community is intentionally visible to other users.

Security

Network traffic uses HTTPS/TLS. Optional API keys use iOS Keychain storage. Access to hosted community and collaboration features requires an authenticated session. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Children

NaturaList is not directed to children under 13, and community features are intended for users able to consent to account-based services. If you believe a child provided personal information, contact us so we can investigate and delete it.

Your Choices And Rights

You can edit or delete local data in the app, stop using Sign in with Apple, delete your hosted NaturaList account, revoke permissions in iOS Settings, and contact us to request access, correction, or deletion where applicable.
